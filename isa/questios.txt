an in-house Data Center (DC) and a private cloud-based Disaster Recovery Center (DRC). The
organization is planning to undertake an Information Security Self-Assessment to evaluate its
current security posture. As part of this initiative, you have been assigned the responsibility of
carrying out the assessment in alignment with the internationally recognized ISO/IEC 27001:2022
standard, focusing on the 93 Annex A controls. This exercise aims to systematically review
existing security measures, identify potential risks or gaps, and support continuous improvement
of the Information Security Management System (ISMS). [10 marks]

Cyber Security Maturity Assessment: A well-established multinational organization operating across more than 300 locations,
supported by a centralized Data Center (DC) and a Disaster Recovery Center (DRC), plans to
undertake a Cybersecurity Maturity Assessment aligned with the NIST Cybersecurity Framework
(CSF). The objective of this assessment is to evaluate the organization’s current cybersecurity
posture across the five core functions of the framework—Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and
Recover—and to systematically analyze and document the maturity level, gaps, and improvement
opportunities to strengthen the overall cybersecurity resilience of the organization. [10 marks]

Building Terms of Reference of an IS Audit: A leading organization operating more than 100 sites, supported by an in-house Data Center (DC)
and a private cloud–based Disaster Recovery Center (DRC), intends to carry out an Information
Systems (IS) Audit. In alignment with internationally recognized standards and frameworks—such
as NIST, ISO/IEC 27001:2022, and CIS—as well as applicable IT policies, bylaws, and regulatory
guidelines issued by the relevant authority, you are required to develop a comprehensive Terms
of Reference (ToR) for the IS Audit. [10 marks]

ransomware readiness assessment, and what major weaknesses might be identified within the
