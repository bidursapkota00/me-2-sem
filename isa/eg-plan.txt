1. Executive Summary & Objective
The objective of this Information Security Audit is to evaluate the adequacy, design, and operating effectiveness of
the organization’s Information Security Management System (ISMS) against the mandatory requirements of ISO/
IEC 27001:2022 (Clauses 4 to 10) and the transitioned information security controls listed in Annex A (Themes A.5
to A.8).
The core goal is to identify gaps, vulnerabilities, and non-conformities prior to the external surveillance audit,
verifying that information security is seamlessly embedded into operational structures.
Key Shift: ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Focus
This audit plan incorporates the restructured control format of the 2022 revision, shifting from 114 controls across 14
domains to 93 controls grouped into 4 distinct themes. Special focus will be placed on newly introduced controls
including threat intelligence, ICT readiness, and cloud data protection.

2. Audit Scope & Boundaries
The scope of this audit encompasses all operational processes, human resources, physical spaces, and technical
frameworks that support the core client platforms:
Infrastructure: Corporate Data Center (HQ), AWS Cloud Infrastructure (Production & Staging), and Remote
Working Endpoints.
Business Functions: Information Technology, Human Resources, Legal & Compliance, Procurement, Software
Engineering, and DevOps.
Physical Boundaries: Head Office (Floors 4, 5, and 6) and the Primary Data Center facility.
3. Audit Methodology
The audit team will gather sufficient, reliable, and relevant evidence through a multi-tiered approach:
Documentation Review: Evaluation of policies, procedures, standards, Risk Assessment Reports, and the
Statement of Applicability (SoA).

5. Categorization of Audit Findings
Discovered issues will be classified uniformly according to risk and conformance severity:
Major Non-Conformity (NC): A systemic breakdown or complete absence of a mandatory clause requirement
or critical Annex A control that directly impacts the integrity of the ISMS.
Minor Non-Conformity (NC): An isolated slip or single programmatic failure to adhere to a documented
procedure or minor control requirement, which does not jeopardize overall system security.
Opportunity for Improvement (OFI): A valid observation of an operational area that is currently compliant, but
could be enhanced to elevate resilience or align with industry best practices.
6. Reporting & Deliverables Timeline
Following conclusion of fieldwork on Day 5, the following reporting cadence will be strictly observed:
Draft Audit Report: Submitted to the CISO within 5 business days post-audit for factual verification and initial
comment.
Corrective Action Plan (CAP): Auditees must submit remediation plans and target resolution dates within 10
business days of receiving the draft report.
Final Audit Report: Issued to Senior Management and the Board Risk Committee within 20 business days
post-audit, containing verified findings and agreed CAP milestones.
